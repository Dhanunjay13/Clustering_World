03 / METHODOLOGY

About the model

How the country groups were created and what they mean.

THE APPROACH

From indicators to groups

  1. Prepare profiles. Average each indicator by country. The source has no explicit year column.
  2. Process indicators. Cap outliers using IQR bounds and fill missing values with training medians.
  3. Scale and reduce. Standardize indicators and retain 12 principal components explaining about 95.55% of variance.
  4. Group countries. K-Means assigns each profile to one of four groups.
INTERPRETING RESULTS

What to keep in mind

Cluster numbers are arbitrary labels, not official rankings of development.

The silhouette score describes separation on the training data. It is not supervised accuracy.

The notebook compares K-Means with five other algorithms. DBSCAN excludes noise from its scores.
